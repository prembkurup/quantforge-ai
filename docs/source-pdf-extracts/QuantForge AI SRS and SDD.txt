1. Introduction
1.1 Project Overview
QuantForge AI is an enterprise-grade quantitative trading platform designed to automatically discover ,
validate, manage, and execute profitable Forex trading strategies using historical market data and live
market analysis.
Unlike traditional trading bots that execute predefined rules, QuantForge AI continuously researches
historical data to identify statistically robust strategies, validates them using rigorous testing methods, and
selects the most appropriate strategy for current market conditions before executing live trades.
The platform is designed to operate entirely on in-house Linux servers while using the OANDA API for live
market data and trade execution.
2. Vision Statement
To build a self-improving quantitative trading platform capable of:
Discovering profitable trading strategies automatically.
Detecting current market conditions.
Selecting the best strategy for each market regime.
Executing trades with disciplined risk management.
Continuously evaluating strategy performance.
Supporting future expansion into stocks, cryptocurrencies, indices, commodities, and futures.

3. Project Objectives
The system shall:
Store more than ten years of historical Forex data.
Support all available Forex currency pairs.
Support multiple timeframes from M1 to Monthly.
Discover profitable strategies automatically.
Validate strategies before deployment.
Detect real-time market regimes.
Select the optimal strategy dynamically.
Execute trades automatically through OANDA.
Manage trading risk.
Produce comprehensive analytical reports.
Operate continuously with minimal manual intervention.

8. Non-Functional Requirements
Performance
Process new candles with minimal latency.
Support concurrent analysis across multiple pairs.
Reliability
Automatic recovery after unexpected failures.
Scheduled database backups.
Audit logging for all critical operations.
Security
Secure API credentials.
Role-based authentication.
Encrypted communication where applicable.
Maintainability
Modular architecture.
Version-controlled documentation and code.
Independent deployment of services.
Scalability
The platform must support additional asset classes and increased workloads without requiring major
architectural changes.
