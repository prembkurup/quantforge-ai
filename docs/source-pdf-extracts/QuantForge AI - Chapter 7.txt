7.1 Purpose
The Research Engine is the intelligence core of QuantForge AI.
Its purpose is to transform historical market data into validated, production-ready trading strategies
through a structured quantitative research pipeline.
Unlike traditional trading systems that rely on manually created strategies, the Research Engine
automatically:
Understands market behaviour
Creates candidate strategies
Optimizes parameters
Validates robustness
Ranks strategies
Publishes approved strategies for production
The Research Engine never executes live trades.
Its responsibility ends when an approved strategy is published.
7.2 Design Principles
The Research Engine follows these principles:
Research must be reproducible.
Every experiment is traceable.
Every strategy is explainable.
Every strategy must be statistically validated.
Research and production remain isolated.

7.19 Outputs
The Research Engine produces:
Approved strategies
Validation reports
Backtest reports
Optimization summaries
Strategy rankings
Research analytics
Deployment candidates
These outputs are consumed by the Strategy Service and, after approval, made available to the Live Trading
platform.
7.20 Guiding Principle
The Research Engine does not attempt to predict the future.
Its purpose is to discover statistically robust trading behaviour from historical data, validate that behaviour
under multiple conditions, and produce explainable strategies that can be monitored, improved, and safely
deployed.
