5.1 Purpose
The purpose of this chapter is to define the Logical Database Model (LDM) for QuantForge AI.
The Logical Database Model describes:
Business entities
Relationships
Ownership
Lifecycle
Data classification
Access patterns
This chapter is independent of PostgreSQL.
No SQL tables are defined here.
The Physical Database Schema will be designed in Chapter 6.

Principle 1
Raw market data is immutable.
Historical candles shall never be modified after validation.
Principle 2
Indicators are derived data.
Indicators shall be calculated by the Research Engine and cached only when necessary.
Principle 3
Every business entity has one owner .
No two services may own the same entity.
Principle 4
Every change must be auditable.
Critical entities must maintain version history.
Principle 5
Research and Production are isolated.
Research data must never directly drive live trading.
Only validated strategies are promoted to production.

5.5 Entity Ownership
Every entity belongs to one service.
Service Owns
Data Service Candles, Ticks, Spread, Instruments
Research ServiceFeatures, Research Runs, Optimization, Backtests
Strategy ServiceStrategy Templates, Strategies, Versions
Trading Service Signals, Orders, Executions, Positions
Risk Service Risk Rules, Exposure, Limits
Reporting ServiceReports, Statistics
API Service Users, Roles, Configuration
Only the owning service may modify an entity.
Other services have read-only access where appropriate.

5.12 Data Access Rules
Rule 1
Research cannot modify production trades.
Rule 2
Trading cannot modify research results.
Rule 3
Risk Engine can reject any order .
Rule 4
Execution Engine never bypasses Risk Engine.
Rule 5
Production Strategy Store contains only approved strategy versions.

5.17 Logical Database Summary
The Logical Database Model defines the ownership, lifecycle, and relationships of all business entities
before any physical database implementation.
The model establishes the following architectural rules:
Raw OHLCV is the permanent source of truth.
Indicators are derived rather than permanently stored.
Research and production remain isolated.
Every entity has a single owner .
Strategies are version-controlled.
Risk management is mandatory for every live trade.
Only validated strategies can enter production.
