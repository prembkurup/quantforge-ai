8.1 Purpose
The Experiment Management System (EMS) is responsible for planning, executing, monitoring, tracking,
comparing, and reproducing quantitative trading research.
Every research activity inside QuantForge AI shall be performed as an Experiment.
Strategies are outputs of experiments.
Experiments are first-class business entities.
8.2 Why an Experiment-Centric Design?
Traditional trading software focuses on strategies.
Professional quantitative research focuses on experiments.
Instead of asking:
"Is this strategy profitable?"
QuantForge AI asks:
Which hypothesis is being tested?
Which data was used?
Which features were used?
Which parameters were explored?
Which validation methods were applied?
Is the result reproducible?

8.6 Research Hypothesis
Every experiment begins with a hypothesis.
Examples:
Trend pullbacks outperform breakouts in trending markets.
ATR exits outperform fixed take-profit.
RSI confirmation improves expectancy.
Mean reversion works better during low volatility.
London session produces higher-quality signals than Asian session.
A hypothesis provides purpose and prevents random research.

8.20 Guiding Principles
Every research activity is an Experiment.
Every experiment starts with a hypothesis.
Every experiment is reproducible.
Every result is traceable.
Failed experiments are valuable learning data.
Strategies are outputs—not the primary object.
Research quality is more important than research speed.
Experiment metadata is never discarded.
8.21 Chapter Summary
The Experiment Management System transforms QuantForge AI from a conventional trading application
into a quantitative research platform.
It provides:
Scientific research workflow
Repeatable experimentation
Structured hypothesis testing
Controlled strategy generation
Complete traceability
Long-term knowledge accumulation
This chapter establishes the foundation for all future research conducted by QuantForge AI.
