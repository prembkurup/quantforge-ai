4.1 Purpose
The purpose of the Enterprise Domain Model is to define the major business concepts, entities,
relationships, and boundaries within QuantForge AI before creating the PostgreSQL database schema.
This chapter does not define physical database tables yet. Instead, it defines the conceptual model that the
database, Python services, APIs, and documentation will be built from.
4.2 Domain Model Overview
QuantForge AI is divided into the following domains:
QuantForge AI

4.15 Domain Relationships
High-level relationships:
Instrument has many Candles
Strategy uses many Plugins
Strategy has many Versions
Strategy Version has many Backtests
Backtest has many Backtest Trades
Strategy Version has many Validation Runs

Live Signal can create Orders

4.17 Core Domain Rules
Rule 1
Raw OHLCV data is the permanent source of truth.
20

--- Page 21 ---
Rule 2
Indicators are derived and not permanently stored by default.
Rule 3
Strategies are composed of reusable plugins.
Rule 4
A strategy must be versioned after approval.
Rule 5
Only approved strategy versions can be used for live trading.
Rule 6
Live trading must always pass through the Risk Management Domain.
Rule 7
Every trade decision must be auditable.
Rule 8
Research and live trading must remain operationally separated.
Rule 9
All OANDA communication must be logged.
Rule 10
No strategy can bypass the kill switch.
4.18 Domain Model Summary
QuantForge AI is not a single trading bot. It is a modular quantitative trading platform made of separate
but connected domains.
21

--- Page 22 ---
The most important design decisions are:
Market data is permanent.
Indicators are derived.
Strategies are plugin-based.
Research is reproducible.
Validation controls deployment.
Risk controls execution.
Every action is auditable.
This domain model will be used as the foundation for the PostgreSQL database design in the next chapter .
