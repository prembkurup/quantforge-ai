1. Architecture Decision AD-002
Strategy Discovery Shall Be Template-Based and Plugin-Driven
QuantForge AI shall not generate random indicator combinations as its main discovery method.
Instead, the platform shall use a structured strategy template framework where strategies are assembled
from reusable components.
2. Reason for Change
Random strategy generation creates many low-quality strategies and increases the risk of overfitting.
Example of weak random discovery:
EMA20 > EMA50
AND RSI > 60
AND MACD > 0
This may work historically by chance, but it may not represent a real market behaviour .
QuantForge AI shall instead build strategies from professional trading components:

5. Plugin-Based Architecture
Each component shall be implemented as a plugin.
A plugin is a self-contained Python class that follows a standard interface.
Example:
classEntryPlugin:
name= "EMA Pullback Entry"
plugin_type= "entry"
defgenerate_signal(self, candles, indicators, params):
pass
This allows QuantForge AI to add new strategy logic without changing the core research engine.
6. Standard Plugin Interface
Every plugin shall define:
plugin_id
plugin_name
plugin_type
description
required_inputs
parameters
default_parameters
compatible_regimes
compatible_timeframes
compatible_pairs
version
status

9. Benefits of the New Design
Better Strategy Quality
Strategies are based on meaningful trading logic, not random combinations.
Lower Overfitting Risk
Each strategy has a clear market purpose.
Easier Debugging
If a strategy fails, we can identify whether the issue is entry, confirmation, exit, or risk.
Easier Expansion
New indicators, entries, exits, and risk models can be added as plugins.
Better Documentation
Each strategy becomes explainable.
Better Strategy Library
Strategies can be grouped by regime, entry type, exit type, and risk model.

13. Parameter Generator
The Parameter Generator shall create parameter combinations for each plugin.
Example:
EMA Fast: 10, 20, 30
EMA Slow: 50, 100, 200
ADX Minimum: 20, 25, 30
ATR SL: 1.5, 2.0, 2.5
ATR TP: 2.0, 3.0, 4.0
The engine shall avoid invalid combinations.
Example:
EMA Fast >= EMA Slow
This shall be rejected.

15. Updated Architecture Principle
QuantForge AI shall follow this principle:
Raw market data is permanent.
Indicators are derived.
Strategies are component-based.
Trading logic is plugin-driven.
Research outputs are reproducible.
Live trading uses only validated strategies.
